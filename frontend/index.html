STEP 01

Candidate & Resume Intake

Resumes uploaded in PDF or DOCX format are securely stored in private local storage outside public roots. SHA-256 checksums prevent tampering.

STEP 02

CSV Bulk Onboarding

Multi-row candidate spreadsheets are validated with preview generation, email deduplication, and atomic confirmation rollback.

STEP 03

Automated Screening

Pre-review checks flag anomalies: missing contact metadata, date inconsistencies, future employment anomalies, and section gaps.

STEP 04

Reviewer Sign-Off

Authorized compliance reviewers examine flagged items, record structured decisions (Approved / Rejected / Needs Info), and write audit logs.
